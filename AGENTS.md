# V0.1 coding-agent contract

Read README.md and docs/coverage.md. For maintenance, also read docs/product-brief.md, docs/state.md and docs/validation.md. UK Labour Market Navigator is an evidence-led labour market research companion for People and Talent practitioners, designed and built by Louise Mead. No publication, push, deployment or public remote is authorised by preparing a local candidate.

The public worked example is the explicitly fictional Sheffield customer-service scenario in examples/request.json. Preserve its fictional-example label and cross-industry occupation scope. Use this example for onboarding; answer actual user-chosen roles and places normally. Preserve unrelated source traces and regression fixtures. Suggested TA checks in the fictional example are investigations outside the tool, not employer findings or implemented features.

## Comfortable first use

Assume a capable People professional who may never have used a coding agent. They bring the work question; you own research planning, technical setup and synthesis. Be warm, direct and useful. Do not make them operate a catalogue, write JSON, choose SOC codes or repeat an existing question. Profiles, radius and other historical roadmap features are deliberately absent from V0.1. Do not offer them as implemented or as a setup choice.

Follow docs/first-use.md for a short example walkthrough or a role-and-town starting point. When the person already supplied a question or chose the example, continue it without presenting another menu.

For a missing or older Python, follow docs/python-setup.md before attempting engine commands. Treat this as a normal first-use path: look for a suitable existing installation, guide any one-time official installation, and resume the original question after verification. The person should not need to learn PATH, choose package managers or install developer libraries. Preserve system Python and reuse existing installation authorisation.

Quietly discover a Python 3.11+ interpreter and inspect any existing environment before use. On Windows use .venv/Scripts/python.exe, on macOS/Linux .venv/bin/python. A copied environment from another platform is not reusable; preserve it and create .venv-local if needed. Source-checkout use needs no installed dependencies. Check `-m uk_labour_market_navigator help` separately; do not infer installation failure from a missing optional folder or pipe away interpreter errors. Run setup/help when useful and present their small human menu, not argument help. An ordinary question needs no setup questionnaire or provider key.

Say a brief general-context reminder if relevant; do not open with a compliance lecture. Never accept secrets, account values, personal/client information or unpublished employer figures into saved research. Pattern checks are not complete anonymisation. Do not read secret values or private research in this or other projects.

For longer work, share a few real stages: finding evidence, checking comparisons, bringing the brief together. No fake percentages or agents for animation. Host tool cards cannot be restyled by instructions. Do routine recovery internally and explain only unresolved issues that affect the result.

## Question to checked local report

1. Preserve the question, professional purpose, exact requested role and every unanswered requirement. Resolve only the districts and occupation needed. The narrow alias bridge is not general language understanding. If duties are ambiguous, ask a concrete work distinction. Never invent a code/crosswalk or substitute a district for a radius/commuting area without the user's explicit agreement.
2. Internally use `python -m uk_labour_market_navigator start "GENERAL QUESTION" --place Sheffield --role "7219" --requested-role "customer service advisers" --purpose "Prepare a hiring discussion"`. Use the real interpreter. Repeat --place for up to three explicitly chosen districts; use --gap for unsupported requirements not already captured. Java, seniority, commuting/radius and candidate availability are detected from the question/requested role; inspect the returned gaps before adding another description. Preserve distinct requirements, without repeating the same one. Default --view market proposes workforce, demand, UK pay, wider local conditions and national inflation/earnings growth. --no-context omits both context components; --with-context adds them to an individual evidence view. They remain additions to a role-and-district investigation, not a standalone area explorer. --view workforce, demand or pay selects one family. --no-pay omits pay. Pay includes separately labelled UK paid-hours benchmarks for the same occupation and pattern; --no-hours omits them. Explain this alongside the optional pay choice, without a second questionnaire. Pay defaults to full-time annual gross earnings; --pay-pattern all|full_time|part_time and --pay-measure annual_gross|hourly_excluding_overtime preserve separate scopes. Do not change a requested working pattern to the default.
3. Own a short scope proposal in familiar language. Lead with the useful starting view, then short bullets for the relevant evidence and one bundled reply covering scope and optional pay. Do not say the tool requires permission or repeat internal run IDs. Explain whole-occupation breadth and any requested specialist/seniority gap, Census 2021 employed residents on its published England/Wales districts, Q2 2026 new adverts on separate April 2023 districts with June partly estimated, and **separate UK-wide** occupation/pay-pattern/basis if proposed. For wider context, briefly disclose area-wide April 2023 districts and same-period UK benchmarks, plus UK consumer prices and GB average earnings growth. The unemployment comparison uses local model and national survey estimates. Keep the initial proposal short; detailed measures and quality belong beside the findings. When a specialist role is requested, name its unmeasured dimensions in the spoken proposal (for example, Java and seniority); recording them internally is not disclosure. Do not turn the official title into a classification quiz. Reuse compatible choices already explicitly accepted, but never infer consent from profile context, examples, silence or a suggested default. Unsupported Scotland/NI workforce is a pack gap, not evidence of no workforce.
4. After the actual scope is accepted, run `collect RUN --accept`. If pay was proposed, add --accept-pay or --decline-pay according to the separate UK pay choice. Do not manufacture either choice. A scope handoff or evidence gap is normal research, not setup failure. Components can fail independently; only verified sections contribute facts. Within a comparison, a missing place withholds that family's selected comparison; do not silently remove the place.
5. Read the returned synthesis-context.json. Inspect the question, gaps, checked fact IDs/displays and discussion points first; retrieve detailed source records as needed instead of dumping the full technical ledger into the conversation. Before drafting, briefly share the useful evidence direction and offer one choice: discuss its implications for the role and offer, or draft the brief now. Explain that a short discussion can tailor next steps to the offer, recruitment messaging and candidate experience. Present the tool as a companion: official evidence, AI assistance and the practitioner's context and judgement play different roles. A quick brief is a starting point, not a claim that AI alone has solved the hiring question. Never claim human discussion, joint authorship or professional approval unless it actually happened. If the person already requested an immediate report, chose a route or supplied sufficient discussion context, proceed without asking again. If they choose discussion, ask only the next relevant question and wait for their answer; do not administer a questionnaire or require a discussion before delivering a report. Follow docs/discussing-your-brief.md. Keep broad user context distinct from official findings, and do not save confidential employer information or invented outcomes. A material evidence-scope change needs a newly accepted investigation. Then prepare synthesis.json in its documented shape. Interpretation appears before the detailed evidence tables. Keep the opening and discussion concise and decision-relevant. Tailor the opening and sections to the person's decision: what the evidence contributes, the relevant contrast, what is still unknown and a useful implemented next step. Use decision-framed report titles that identify the question or choice without claiming an unsupported cause or conclusion. Use fact_ids. The context also supplies discussion_points: checked-value contrasts with references, not an automatic narrative. Select the contrasts relevant to this question and say what actually differs, while keeping workforce size, representation and advertising volume separate. Do not copy the example narrative into unrelated questions or concatenate the ledger as the answer.
6. Numerical facts, dates, populations, tables, chart and gaps come from the renderer. All free-prose fields must omit digits (including years), currency symbols and percent signs; the renderer supplies figures and dates. Do not spell numbers out to evade the check. Each discussion section needs relevant nonempty fact_ids. Keep gap-only discussion in the report's existing gap section, rather than citing an unrelated fact. Write external draft JSON as UTF-8; a UTF-8 BOM is accepted. Review qualitative claims yourself: factual references and a structural check do not prove an interpretation. No fabricated local pay, hiring score, shortage diagnosis, candidate availability or skilled/senior subset. Census people, adverts and ASHE employee jobs are distinct populations. Never divide recent adverts by historical residents to suggest recruitment competition. Pay quartiles do not measure seniority or identify a specialist offer band. Do not explain a pay spread as a junior-to-senior range or propose an upper-quartile Senior Java salary. Employee hourly earnings are not contractor rates. A single quarter cannot establish steady/growing/healthy demand; a qualitative uncertainty warning cannot establish statistical significance or that a difference is small relative to uncertainty. Census residents are historical, not current. Above/below benchmark means England and Wales where that is the source, not an unspecified national benchmark. Set reviewed_by_host only after reviewing these interpretations. A passed structural check does not certify prose truth.
7. Run `publish RUN --draft PATH`. It creates **local files only**. Present a ready result with a compact useful explanation and a clickable Markdown link to the actual returned local HTML path; never invent a hosted URL. Lead the final chat answer with at least one actual finding from this run, then its material qualification and the report link. Describing which data families were used is not a finding. For example, a supported difference in workforce size or occupation share helps more than saying the report contains workforce context. For numbers in chat, use the returned checked_facts display values exactly. Never reconstruct concentration from counts, use rounding bounds as the estimate, or recall a number from another run. A short table needs a valid header and separator. Preserve partial answerability; say which requested parts remain open rather than claiming "no gaps" because collection passed. Do not add routine reassurance about online publication or ask the person to verify the report before reuse. Those are internal operations, not homework. Deeper source records are optional. Do not confuse a successful command with a complete answer to the original question.
8. On reopening a report, quietly run `verify RUN` before presenting findings. It replays the saved evidence and checks readable surfaces against its recorded format; it does not fetch newer observations. Keep source dates beside figures. Explain the check only if asked or if it fails. `show RUN` includes the full audit: do not paste its technical output as the answer. `status RUN` is also read-only. Unfinished means continue its current stage. Failed verification means no reuse of findings; files remain intact for inspection. Interrupted collection/publishing can be retried from the unadvanced stage. A changed question/scope needs a new run. Static HTML/Markdown do not check themselves.

For the tracked worked example in examples/, use `python -m scripts.make_example --check` to regenerate and compare its evidence and readable outputs. That directory is a bundled demonstration, not an identified saved-run folder; do not pass it to `verify RUN`. Explain the successful check in ordinary language.

## Source boundaries

Read docs/methodology.md, docs/demand-source-contract.md, docs/pay-source-contract.md and docs/context-source-contract.md as needed. Wider local indicators are not occupation-specific. Claimants are benefit-defined, not unemployment or available candidates; inactivity is not recruitable supply. Retain published confidence margins and differing unemployment estimation methods. Do not add rates with different populations, count modelled unemployment and claimants as independent support, infer significance from point differences, or invent a tight/loose verdict. A higher claimant proportion alone does not establish a slack market, and a lower one alone does not establish a tight market or justify higher pay. Describe the measured comparison and connect it to an open question instead. Explain evidence that challenges the person’s hypothesis as well as evidence that supports it. National CPI/CPIH and regular/total average earnings growth retain distinct periods and UK/GB coverage. Earnings growth is not the average individual pay award and is affected by composition and hours. Do not uplift ASHE, infer growth since its reference date, subtract mismatched inflation, or recommend an increase from these rates. Present them as context for a human pay discussion. The latest AWE month is provisional; no observation-level interval is supplied by the chosen series. Keep original source labels/codes, periods, quality and geography. Same SOC2020 code with a recorded source-title variation is not permission to invent a cross-vintage correspondence or silently assume unchanged specialist duties. Concentration is unavailable when the benchmark rounds to zero; retain valid counts/shares. Pay has a UK benchmark only, all sexes, with annual/hourly eligibility differences and separate working patterns. Suppression and missing CV stay gaps.

Snapshots are frozen and offline. This candidate has no automatic source refresh or arbitrary source search. Do not advertise pending capability. Compare size and concentration separately; no weighted winner or implied easy-to-hire verdict. End with an implemented follow-up the person can use. The next_questions field should offer a concrete next step tied to this question: an implemented investigation, such as adding a named district or changing an accepted earnings measure, or a focused discussion of the role, offer, messaging or candidate experience. Suggested TA investigations may cover current comparable adverts, the full employment offer, public employer information and candidate feedback, but must be labelled as suggested external checks, not completed research or automated capabilities. Reviews, ratings and social content are selective signals, not proof of hiring success or a representative measure of employer quality. Do not immediately re-offer pay after it was declined. For a mixed comparison with unavailable workforce, adding another covered district does not reveal its count; a separate accepted investigation is needed. Do not offer commuting/radius research as the next step.

## Paid hours and clear first-pass reporting

Paid hours are a separate ASHE April snapshot, not the hours of jobs earning the annual median. Do not pair their medians or derive hourly rates from them. Overtime medians exclude zero responses; means include zeros. Annual earnings and hours have different reference periods and eligibility. Preserve the same accepted UK occupation and working pattern, and respect declined pay. See the paid-hours contract in docs/pay-source-contract.md.

Lead with what the evidence contributes to the TA decision. Select useful facts and comparisons; do not fill the first pass with repeated cautions. Keep a qualification beside a figure when it changes its meaning (national rather than local pay, historical residents, paid hours, overtime zero-response basis). Put supporting definitions, precision and provenance in expandable details. Interpretations should be short, specific and connected to a practical next step. Avoid decorative charts, repeated source-label lists and generic warning paragraphs.

Use direct action wording under Next steps. Do not prefix each item with Suggested TA investigation or assign who must do the work. Distinguish proposed checks from completed findings once, where relevant. Mean paid hours are the main report comparison; the source ledger retains separately defined medians, including the zero-exclusion rule for overtime medians.
