Counts of covered districts describe the source dimensions, not a promise of a usable observation for every role. Advertising contains 133,853 observations, 74,274 suppressed, including published aggregates; no suppressed value becomes zero. Source releases contain 412 occupation codes. Counts/shares can remain available where concentration cannot be calculated because the England/Wales benchmark share rounds to zero (78 occupations).

Compare one to three explicitly selected districts. Within workforce or advertising, if a selected district is missing or incompatible, the whole selected comparison for that family is withheld. Other independent families continue. No place is silently dropped, no unavailable nation is replaced, and there is no weighted winner or ranking score.

District identity does not establish identical boundaries across sources. The report preserves each release's geography. UK earnings appear once, not as differing local salaries. A postcode radius, commuting area or metropolitan region cannot silently become a district.

Annual earnings cover the tax year ending 5 April 2025 and employee jobs held more than a year; hourly earnings cover the pay period including 30 April 2025. Their eligibility definitions differ. All/full-time/part-time remain separate; no annualisation is performed.

There is no measured Java subset, seniority band, current available-candidate count, employer-specific competition, causal hiring diagnosis or current live vacancy total. A broader accepted view retains these unanswered requirements.

Wider local comparisons are withheld per indicator if any selected district or its UK comparator is unusable. Other indicators can continue. The unemployment comparison explicitly uses a local model and a national survey estimate. Prices and earnings growth are separate national context, not individual pay awards or salary adjustments. See [wider-context definitions](context-source-contract.md).

Paid hours use ASHE Table 14 total/basic/overtime weekly paid hours, with separately checked medians and means for the accepted UK occupation and working pattern. The April snapshot is not paired with annual earnings. Overtime medians exclude zero responses; means include them. Unpaid hours are not measured. Each estimate needs its own usable CV; other facts remain available if a cell is suppressed.
