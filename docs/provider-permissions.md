# Adzuna access and permitted use

Your intended use and your agreement with Adzuna determine which access is appropriate. An API key or a higher request allowance does not, by itself, grant permission for ongoing organisational research.

This guide summarises the public [API terms](https://developer.adzuna.com/docs/terms_of_service), [overview](https://developer.adzuna.com/overview) and [search documentation](https://developer.adzuna.com/docs/search) reviewed on 12 September 2026. Check the current provider terms before choosing an account or changing how you use it; this project cannot grant provider permission.

| Intended use | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Personal research | Listed as permitted by the reviewed API terms. Use your own account within its conditions and allowance. |
| Organisational evaluation | The terms describe a 14-day trial for other commercial, government or academic organisational uses, limited to testing coverage, quality and usability. |
| Ongoing workplace, client or other organisational research outside the listed permitted purposes | Obtain written consent from Adzuna. A licence agreement may be required. |
| More API requests | Ask Adzuna about an allowance increase. Request limits and usage rights are separate. |
| Public advert listings | Separate attribution and branding requirements apply. This connector does not publish listings. |

The organisational evaluation period is not a universal deadline after which every user must pay. Equally, a personal/academic account label does not establish permission for every workplace or client use. If your intended activity is not clearly covered, confirm it with Adzuna before searching.

## How connection records your choice

The connector supports two permission choices: personal research, or provider permission for the intended use. It does not offer an organisational trial mode or infer permission from the account's age. Connecting stores credentials locally without testing the account or accepting provider terms on your behalf.

Searches are individually confirmed and limited to one page, without automatic retries. Provider records stay outside official saved reports and are not cached or exported by the connector. Results displayed in a coding agent may remain in its conversation history; local non-retention does not erase that history.

See [connecting and using Adzuna](adzuna.md) for the practical steps, and the [connector contract](adzuna-connector.md) for technical safeguards and verification limits.
