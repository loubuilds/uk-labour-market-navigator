# How a brief is assembled and checked

The coding agent understands the question, retains the requested role and gaps, proposes scope and writes the explanation. Local deterministic Python resolves qualified identities, extracts bundled official observations, calculates supported measures, records literal source cells and verifies saved reports. No model-generated crosswalk or numerical estimate is admitted.

## Separate populations

Census workforce describes employed residents aged 16+ at Census 2021, not current candidates or workplace jobs. Counts round to five; shares round to one decimal place. Concentration is local occupation share divided by the published England/Wales share. Its range reflects rounding only. If the denominator rounds to zero, count/share remain and concentration is unavailable. It is never replaced by an epsilon.

ONS advertising describes new online adverts in April-June 2026, assigned to whole SOC2020 occupations and April 2023 districts. June is partly imputed; source uncertainty increased from November 2025. Suppressed cells stay missing. This is neither live vacancies nor distinct employers. Dividing this flow by Census resident counts from a different period and population would not establish recruitment competition.

ASHE Table 14 describes actual UK employee-job earnings in 2025, not district offers or advertised salaries. The user must separately agree to the UK-wide geography. Each percentile needs its matching CV; missing/suppressed estimates or quality and CV above the qualified limit supply no fact. Annual and hourly populations differ. See [the full source contract](pay-source-contract.md).

## Scope and evidence gaps

Shared, hash-pinned identities resolve names without loading numerical packs. Their registry is reproduced from qualified classification and source-geography references. Each evidence family checks its own source contract. A component failure or unsupported selected comparison withholds that family while other verified families remain usable. Failure of the common identity trust root prevents an investigation because its dimensions cannot be trusted.

A selected multi-district comparison must have all selected observations within that family; wider local conditions apply this rule per indicator. The engine does not silently reduce it. Unknown requirements remain gaps, including skill, seniority, radius or unsupported geography. The bounded keyword helper is not general natural-language understanding; the host must preserve additional requirements explicitly.

## Verification and recovery

New runs carry an application/run identity. Checking is read-only: unrelated folders, unknown identities and invalid completed reports yield no findings without changing files. Unfinished runs remain unfinished. Collection and rendering use atomic file replacement and advance the stage last; retry the same step after an interrupted write. This is proportionate local crash recovery, not a multi-user transactional store. Symlink/junction paths are refused.

Saved evidence is independently replayed against the installed qualified source, not trusted because the report carries its own hashes. Readable HTML/Markdown are reconstructed and compared as well. A failed recheck withdraws the result logically; existing static files remain for inspection and must not be reused. Editing source code or its trusted pins is outside this gate's threat model. It is not a digital signature or an authenticity certificate for arbitrary downloads.

AI/host prose is structurally validated, kept free of new numerical claims and linked to available fact IDs. This does **not** prove its truth or rule out qualitative overreach. The host must review it. Active Markdown/images/links and obvious private input patterns are rejected or escaped. These checks are not a semantic fact checker, malware scanner or complete anonymiser.

Source editions are frozen. A source refresh requires maintainer qualification and release validation; a saved report does not become current when reopened. Public readers can inspect original source archives and reproduce the extraction offline using [the validation route](validation.md).

## Wider context

Area-wide claimant, employment, inactivity and model-based unemployment indicators retain separate populations and same-period UK comparisons. Missing local or benchmark observations withhold the affected indicator comparison. Claimants are not unemployment; modelled unemployment uses claimant information; inactivity does not measure availability. Rates and confidence margins are read from the original published cells, and only same-month claimant proportion change is calculated.

UK CPI/CPIH inflation and GB whole-economy average earnings growth supply separate dated context. Different periods are not subtracted, and historical occupation earnings are never inflated into a purported current salary. Average earnings growth is not an individual pay award. See the [context contracts and source traces](context-source-contract.md).
