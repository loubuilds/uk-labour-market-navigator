# Wider conditions for a pay or hiring discussion

A role-and-district brief can include wider local conditions and national prices and earnings growth. These help test the explanation behind a hiring question and put a pay discussion in context. They do not diagnose an employer’s recruitment results or calculate an appropriate increase.

| View | What you can learn | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claimant proportion and annual change | Benefit-defined claimants relative to the working-age population, compared with the UK in the same month | Unemployment, available candidates or an occupation-specific shortage |
| Employment and inactivity | The district’s broad working-age employment pattern, with published confidence margins | Who wants, can take or is qualified for this vacancy |
| Model-based unemployment | A local unemployment estimate with its confidence margin, alongside a same-period UK survey estimate | A definitive tight/loose label; the local and national methods differ |
| CPIH and CPI inflation | Changes in UK consumer prices over the displayed year | Sheffield living costs, every household’s experience or the right salary increase |
| Average earnings growth | Growth in regular and total average weekly earnings across Great Britain | The average individual pay award, this occupation’s growth or an updated ASHE salary |

The agent proposes this broader context with the occupation investigation. It is included in a new general market brief by default, and you can ask to leave it out. Maintainer commands use `--no-context`, or `--with-context` to add it to an individual evidence view. These are additions to the role-and-district workflow; there is no standalone area explorer or arbitrary time-series browser.

Local figures and the UK comparator retain the same reference period. Claimants are shown for July 2026 and compared with July 2025. The annual survey/model window is April 2025–March 2026. Prices cover the twelve months to July 2026; earnings growth compares April–June 2026 with April–June 2025. All are frozen source editions, not live indicators.

The local tables explain percentage points and confidence margins. Missing or unreliable observations stay gaps. If one selected district lacks an indicator, that indicator’s comparison is withheld for the chosen set; other usable indicators and evidence families continue. Northern Ireland has claimant coverage but no local employment, inactivity or model-based unemployment in this pack.

Lower claimant proportions can be relevant to a labour-supply discussion, but alone cannot establish hiring difficulty or justify a particular salary. Consider evidence that challenges the hypothesis as well as evidence that supports it. National prices and earnings growth can motivate questions about review timing and purchasing power, alongside current matched pay evidence, candidate feedback and professional judgement. The tool does not subtract mismatched rates, inflate an old salary or claim an individual has received the national average increase.
