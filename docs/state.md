# Public preview status

UK Labour Market Navigator **0.1.0rc6** is a public preview for evaluation. The [live fictional example](https://loubuilds.github.io/uk-labour-market-navigator/) is available without installation.

The [product scope](product-brief.md) and [coverage matrix](coverage.md) describe implemented capabilities. [Methodology](methodology.md), [advertising](demand-source-contract.md) [earnings](pay-source-contract.md) and [wider context](context-source-contract.md) document the evidence definitions and limitations.

The [validation route](validation.md) checks tests, source reproduction and independent traces, saved-report integrity, example consistency, packaging and five fresh offline installed journeys. Each run creates a local receipt with its revision and results. Public CI results are available in [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/loubuilds/uk-labour-market-navigator/actions); check the result for the revision you use.

The release workflow has passed on Windows/Python 3.12, Linux/Python 3.11 and macOS/Python 3.14, including the paid-hours and first-use changes. Check the CI result for the revision you use. Automated checks do not certify AI interpretation or conversational usefulness. Optional Adzuna tests use synthetic responses and do not establish live account access.

Reports place interpretation before detailed tables and keep original figures, source periods and inspectable records. Earlier saved reports retain their recorded rendering; see [architecture](architecture.md). Source editions are frozen, and reopening a report does not refresh its observations.
