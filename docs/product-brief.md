# Current product scope

This document defines the current V0.1 product scope. Changes to evidence coverage or interpretation require the corresponding source contracts and verification to be updated.

V0.1 is an open-source labour-market research companion for People and Talent teams: workforce, demand, separately accepted UK official earnings and separately scoped paid hours, wider local indicators, national price and average earnings growth context, AI-written interpretation and traceability. Up to three districts. The [README](../README.md), [coverage matrix](coverage.md) and [worked example](worked-example.md) describe current behaviour.

Profiles, radius/commuting, area explorer, discovery, arbitrary national time-series queries, arbitrary research, hosted applications and extra report formats are deliberately deferred. They are not available in this version.
